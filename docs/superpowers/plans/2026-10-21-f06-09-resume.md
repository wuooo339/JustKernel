# F06-09 Pause, Resume and Late Results

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline. Steps use checkbox (`[ ]`).

**Goal:** Manually resume a PAUSED cluster without rerunning interrupted work, and archive a late result bound to the original interrupted task/job while keeping both terminal states intact.

**Architecture:** `ResumeService` resumes only PAUSED clusters and can append a `late_completion` TestRecord directly to the records repository. It never updates the interrupted task/job or starts new work. Late records are excluded from `is_full_pass`, so they cannot automatically become baseline/best evidence.

**Spec:** `SPEC/13-generation-and-iteration-contract.md` §8.

## Global Constraints

- Resume only from PAUSED; COMPLETED/STOPPED/FAILED never resume.
- No automatic task/job rerun on resume.
- Late result requires an INTERRUPTED task and matching INTERRUPTED job.
- Late record keeps original task/job ids and does not mutate their states.
- Late records are not automatically baseline/best evidence.
- No old-code imports/tests.
