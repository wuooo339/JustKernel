# F07-05 Redaction, Export and Download ACL

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline. Steps use checkbox (`[ ]`).

**Goal:** Add centralized redaction, human-only redacted export/report/source, and object-level record-download authorization.

**Architecture:** `platform/security/redactor.py` recursively replaces registered secrets/private roots. `ApiContext` carries a redactor; export routes compose DTOs then redact. Download routes require `X-Human` or task worker/mentor ownership.

**Spec:** `SPEC/10-configuration-security-observability.md` §2; `SPEC/09-api-and-frontend.md` §9.

## Global Constraints

- Secrets shorter than 8 chars are not redacted.
- Private roots and registered secrets never appear in export/report/source text.
- Human export requires explicit header; worker downloads require task ownership.
- No DB rows/private paths in responses.
- No old-code imports/tests.
