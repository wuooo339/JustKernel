# F06-08 Convergence, BestSelection and Report

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans inline. Steps use checkbox (`[ ]`).

**Goal:** Validate a pending best selection with final full-PASS evidence, converge the cluster, attach the best/report, and complete it; failed final validation leaves the cluster and prior best untouched.

**Architecture:** `ConvergenceService` composes `BestSelectionService`, `ReportService`, and `ClusterService`. It validates final evidence before mutating the cluster, writes the report, then applies best/report and completes. ClusterService gains explicit best/report/complete commands.

**Spec:** `SPEC/07-testing-and-profile.md` §7; `SPEC/13-generation-and-iteration-contract.md` §6.

## Global Constraints

- Final validation requires full executed TEST PASS.
- Failure never overwrites best or completes the cluster.
- Report and best must agree and be attached before completion.
- COMPLETED requires best candidate and report.
- No old-code imports/tests.
