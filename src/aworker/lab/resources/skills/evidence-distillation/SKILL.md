---
name: evidence-distillation
description: Distill passed experiments into conditional, evidence-linked experience without inventing gains or generalization.
---

Read the complete result, code identity and input-pack identity.
Separate correctness, timing, source review and official scoring.
Before record_experience, identify a real passed evidence_job and exact tested code.
State conditions, the implementation change, observations and limitations.
A correctness pass alone does not prove speedup. A same-operator observation does not
prove cross-operator transfer; label transfer ideas as hypotheses needing new tests.
Other teams may read and question evidence. Never erase failures or slower cases.
